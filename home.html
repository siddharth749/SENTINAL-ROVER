Security patrolling in large facilities today relies almost entirely on manual guard rounds, which are inconsistent, fatiguing over long shifts, and leave blind spots during low-visibility hours. SENTINEL is a low-cost, ESP32-driven rover that can patrol a defined route or be remotely piloted, stream live video, and flag anomalies — reducing the load on human security staff without replacing their judgment.

What SENTINEL Does

A 4-motor differential-drive rover controlled from a browser-based console over WiFi — live camera feed, joystick or D-pad driving, and real-time telemetry (battery, speed, obstacle distance) reported back from the unit as it moves.
